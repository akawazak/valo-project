How it works

Night Market offers are personal to your account. VantaVault shows the offers your signed-in Riot Client already has, so you can look through them on your desktop. Nothing is bought for you; purchases still happen in VALORANT.

Also in the store view

  • Daily offers with a reset timer
  • Featured bundles and accessories
  • Your VP and other balances
  • Wishlist matches when a skin you want shows up

Questions

Does it show Night Market when it is not running?
No. The section appears when Riot is running Night Market for your account.
Can it reveal or reroll offers?
No. It shows the offers you already have; it cannot change them.
Is it free?
Yes. VantaVault is free and open source.
