How it works

Each stat comes from your saved matches. For example, aim uses headshot rate and damage per round, and entry uses how often you get the first kill. Hover any stat in the app to see exactly how it is measured.

What you can see

  • A playstyle chart with your strongest areas
  • Your play type, like Fragger or Support
  • Recent form over your last matches
  • Best games, best agents and best maps

Questions

Is this an MMR estimate?
No. VantaVault does not estimate hidden MMR; it compares the stats in your own matches.
How many matches does it need?
It uses your saved history and gets more accurate as it grows.
Is it free?
Yes. VantaVault is free and open source.
