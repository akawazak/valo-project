WHAT IT SHOWS

The information that matters before you open the game.

01

Offers with the details attached

Each daily offer keeps its artwork, weapon type, edition tier, price, variants, and ownership context together instead of splitting them across separate pages.

02

Bundles as actual collections

Featured bundles include their hero art and the items inside them. VantaVault reads current Riot data and uses the public content catalog for artwork rather than maintaining bundle-specific hardcoded images.

03

Refreshes at the right time

Store data is cached for responsiveness, refreshed when its rotation changes, and can still be refreshed manually when you need to confirm the newest state.

04

Multiple account support

Keep separate local sessions and switch the selected account without repeatedly typing credentials. Each account's storefront remains separate.

What VantaVault does not do

VantaVault does not buy items, alter prices, inject into VALORANT, or unlock cosmetics. It displays information available to your authenticated account and provides controls supported by Riot's client services.
