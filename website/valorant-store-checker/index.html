How the store checker works

VantaVault reads the information exposed by your active local Windows VALORANT session. That means store and wallet information is only available while the Riot Client is running and signed into the same account. It is not a public store predictor, does not guess future offers, and does not replace the game client.

What you can check

  • Daily storefront skin offers and current prices
  • Featured bundles, accessories, upgrades, variants, and balances
  • Night Market offers when they are active
  • Owned cosmetics and wishlist matches alongside your loadout

Questions

Is this an official Riot app?
No. VantaVault is an independent, open-source companion and is not endorsed by Riot Games.
Does it work when VALORANT is closed?
Store-specific information requires an active local Riot Client session.
Does it sell skins or give discounts?
No. It only shows information already available to your account.
