How the store checker works

VantaVault reads the information your signed-in Riot Client already shares on your PC. Store and wallet details are available while the Riot Client is running and signed in to the same account. It does not predict future offers and does not replace the game client.

What you can check

  • Daily skin offers and current prices
  • Featured bundles, accessories, levels, variants and your balances
  • Night Market offers when they are active
  • Owned cosmetics and wishlist matches next to your loadout

Questions

Is this an official Riot app?
No. VantaVault is an independent, open-source companion and is not endorsed by Riot Games.
Does it work when the Riot Client is closed?
Store information needs an active, signed-in Riot Client on your PC.
Does it sell skins or give discounts?
No. It only shows offers already available to your account.
