Backgrounds from the app

Built on your own Riot Client. Kept on your own PC.

VantaVault reads what your signed-in Riot Client already shares on your computer. It doesn't touch the game itself.

  • Open sourceEvery line is on GitHub.
  • No injection, no memory readingIt never hooks into or reads VALORANT's game process.
  • Logins in Windows Credential ManagerSaved Riot sessions use the same protected storage as Windows itself.
  • Your history stays with youMatches are stored on your PC, for as long as you choose.

VantaVault is an independent project and isn't endorsed by Riot Games. Background art is from Riot Games' official VALORANT media wallpapers, the same themes the app offers.
