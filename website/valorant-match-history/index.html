Private match review on your desktop

VantaVault presents account and match information in one desktop view instead of requiring you to jump between screens. It stores saved match data locally and offers configurable retention. The information available can depend on the active Riot session and the fields Riot exposes at the time.

What you can review

  • Current rank, RR progression, rank history, and lifetime peak
  • Locally stored match history, win rate, and available round analytics
  • Agent and map performance where the active session provides it
  • Profiles alongside the rest of your VantaVault account view

Questions

Is every match guaranteed to appear?
No. Availability depends on the current Riot session and data fields exposed to the app.
Where is match data stored?
VantaVault stores its saved match data locally on your device, with configurable retention.
Does it provide coaching or alter match results?
No. It is a companion for viewing available account information, not a game modification.
