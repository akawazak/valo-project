Private match review on your desktop

VantaVault shows your account and match information in one desktop view instead of making you jump between screens. It stores saved match data on your PC with retention you control. What is available depends on your Riot session and the data Riot shares at the time.

What you can review

  • Current rank, RR progression, rank history and peak
  • Saved match history, win rate and round-by-round review
  • Agent and map performance
  • Session recaps that group a night of games

Questions

Is every match guaranteed to appear?
No. It depends on your Riot session and the data Riot shares with the app.
Where is match data stored?
On your own PC, for as long as you choose.
Does it alter match results?
No. It is a companion for viewing your account information, not a game modification.
