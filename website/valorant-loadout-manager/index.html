One place for cosmetic choices

A loadout is more than one skin. VantaVault brings the choices that belong together into one desktop view, so you can compare variants and save a complete setup. It works with the cosmetics your account owns; it does not unlock, create or trade items.

What you can organize

  • Weapons, skins, chromas, variants and buddies
  • Player cards, titles, sprays and expressions
  • Named presets you can reuse, import and export
  • Presets linked to agents, applied when you lock them in

Questions

Can this give me skins I do not own?
No. VantaVault only manages cosmetics your account already owns.
Does it change settings during a match?
It applies changes when the client allows it and does not get around game restrictions.
Is the app free?
Yes. VantaVault is open source and the Windows download is free.
