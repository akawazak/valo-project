One place for cosmetic choices

A loadout is more than a single skin. VantaVault brings the cosmetic choices that belong together into an organized desktop view, so you can compare variants and save a complete setup. The app works with the cosmetics your account owns; it does not unlock, create, or trade items.

What you can organize

  • Weapons, skins, chromas, variants, and buddies
  • Player cards, titles, sprays, and expressions
  • Saved preset names and reusable cosmetic combinations
  • Import and export of presets for your own backup or setup

Questions

Can this give me skins I do not own?
No. VantaVault only manages cosmetics that are already available to your account.
Does this change settings while I am in a match?
Apply changes when the client allows it; the app does not bypass game restrictions.
Is the app free?
Yes. VantaVault is open source and the Windows download is free.
