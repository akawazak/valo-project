Pick a round to see its kill feed, plants and a replay on the map.

Economy

Your loadout value and your team’s and enemy’s credits for the round.

Map patterns

Where you win fights, where you keep dying, and repeat positions.

Find what changed the match

Round review marks turning points and shows takeaways, like losing rounds after dying first or dying in the same spot again. The Map tab gathers every fight from the match, so you can filter to your kills or deaths and see the pattern.

What you can review

  • A round picker with wins and losses for both halves
  • Kill feed with weapons and timings
  • A replay of player positions on the map
  • Fight locations, repeat positions and round impact on the Map tab

Questions

Which matches can I review?
Matches saved in your VantaVault history, as far as Riot shares the round data.
Is the replay a video?
No. It draws recorded positions and events on the map; nothing is recorded from your screen.
Does it work for other players?
It reviews your own saved matches.
