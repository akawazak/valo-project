How it works

VantaVault looks at the matches you played together in your saved history and counts the trades between you: when one of you died and the other got the kill back soon after. It only covers 5v5 matches you both played.

What you can see

  • Games together and wins
  • Avenges, traded first deaths and assists between you
  • Your best agent pairs with win rates
  • The latest trades, with map and round

Questions

Does my duo need the app?
No. It works from the matches in your own saved history.
Which modes count?
5v5 matches you played together.
Can I compare with anyone?
You can compare with teammates from your own matches.
