# Theme artwork sources

These optimized wallpapers come from Riot Games' official [VALORANT Media](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/media/) wallpaper packs:

- Evolution: Episode 7 key art
- China Launch: China launch key art
- Omen & Outlaw and Cypher Reborn: Episode 8 Act I wallpaper pack
